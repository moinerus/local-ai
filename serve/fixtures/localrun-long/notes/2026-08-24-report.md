# Report, 24 August 2026: summary formatting

## What was broken

`report.summary` printed worker totals in insertion order, so two runs over the
same log could order the workers differently and a diff between two reports
showed changes that were not there.

## What changed

`report.py` now sorts the worker names before rendering, and the slowest-batch
lines zero-pad the batch number to four digits so the column lines up.

## How it was verified

Ran the summary over the same log twice and diffed the output: identical.
Checked a batch number under 1000 renders as `0412`.
